Host-provided interfaces and resources (not bundled copies)

The production output imports obsidian, @codemirror/state, and
@codemirror/view as externals. Moment and icons requested through the Obsidian
API are supplied by the host. These uses do not mean Knomo distributes the
Obsidian application or claims ownership of its resources. The MIT license
of the obsidian npm API typings is not the license of the Obsidian application.
Host components remain governed by their applicable terms. If copies of these
components are added to a distribution, review and include their own notices.

Original embedded SVGs and the Knomo logo are maintainer-authored material
under the Knomo license described in LICENSING.md; trademark use is addressed
separately in BRANDING.md. Screenshots can depict separately owned host
interfaces and third-party marks; no rights in that content are granted here.

Development tools and source packages

General development/test tools such as TypeScript, esbuild, jsdom, and
fake-indexeddb do not contribute code to the reviewed production output.
Packages present only in the lockfile are not automatically bundled runtime.
If a source archive also includes third-party tool or dependency files, preserve
the notices required for those files as well; this runtime inventory alone is
not sufficient for an archive with additional third-party contents.
