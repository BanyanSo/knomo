## Knomo 1.12.9

### New features

- Image preview: Double-click an image to enlarge it, then drag to pan. On mobile, use double-tap or two-finger pinch; panning an enlarged image does not switch images. Switching images resets the zoom.
- Retry failed image loads directly in the preview.

### Improvements

- Mobile search: Update totals and load-more labels without rebuilding cards, preserving scroll position, loaded images, and rendering already in progress.
- Search and counts: Reuse prepared filters within each query and cached counts while queries and the index remain unchanged. Cancel outdated scans when searches change or views close.
- Image preview: Reuse decoded images within a bounded cache of the current and adjacent images, and clear the cache when the preview closes.
- Random reunion: Show up to 10 memos per group, including image-only and link-only notes. Prefer content outside the last three groups while reducing unnecessary sampling work.
- Review status: Displaying random results does not mark them reviewed. A successful open or explicit mark lowers their selection weight for the next three calendar days.
- Shuffle day: Balance selection across available time ranges, favor richer days moderately, and avoid recently shown dates when other candidates are available.
- Review views: Keep the current random group or selected day when returning to the page; use Shuffle to choose new results.
- Sidebar: Reuse unchanged tag trees and update statistics in place, reducing unnecessary rebuilding during pagination and refresh.
- Task lists: Reduce repeated parsing and checkbox scans when rendering long or nested task lists and syncing task states.
- Remove unused tag-aggregation and sampling utilities, and simplify outdated tests while retaining useful behavioral coverage.

### Fixes

- Wait until input-method composition ends before querying mobile search, and discard pending searches when the search page closes.
- Prevent delayed counts from adding a load-more button to an empty mobile search.
- Ensure double-clicking an enlarged preview restores the full image, while keeping clicks distinct from dragging.
- Cancel pending review loads when leaving a review page, preventing delayed results from being applied after navigation.
- Keep the selected day's content available when review history cannot be read or saved, and report the history failure.

### 新增功能

- 图片预览：双击图片放大后可拖动查看细节；移动端支持双击和双指缩放，放大后的平移不会切换图片。切换图片时恢复完整预览。
- 图片加载失败时，可在预览中直接重试。

### 改进

- 移动搜索：更新总数和加载更多文案时保留卡片，避免重新渲染，保留滚动位置、已加载图片和正在进行的分帧渲染。
- 搜索与计数：在单次查询中复用已处理的筛选条件，在查询与索引未变化时复用计数结果；搜索条件变化或视图关闭时取消过期扫描。
- 图片预览：复用已解码图片，仅缓存当前图片及邻图，关闭预览时清理缓存，控制连续浏览时的资源占用。
- 随机重逢：每组最多展示 10 条 Memo，包含纯图片和纯链接记录；优先选择最近三批之外的内容，并减少不必要的抽样工作。
- 回看状态：展示随机结果不会标记已回看；成功打开或显式标记后才记录回看，并在接下来三个日历日内降低抽中权重。
- 往日漫游：在可用年代段之间平衡选择，对内容较丰富的日期给予适度偏好；仍有其他候选时，优先避开近期已展示的日期。
- 回看页面：返回随机重逢或往日漫游时保留当前结果，点击“换一批”才重新选择。
- 侧栏：复用未变化的标签树并原位更新统计，减少分页和刷新时不必要的重建。
- 任务列表：减少长列表、嵌套任务列表渲染和任务状态同步时的重复解析与复选框扫描。
- 删除未使用的标签聚合和抽样工具入口，精简过时测试并保留有效行为覆盖。

### 修复

- 移动搜索等待输入法选字结束后再发起查询，关闭搜索页面时取消尚未提交的查询。
- 防止晚到计数在空搜索提示页生成无效的“加载更多”按钮。
- 确保放大图片后再次双击可恢复完整预览，避免普通点击被误判为拖动。
- 离开回看页面时取消尚未完成的加载，避免导航后继续应用晚到结果。
- 回顾历史读取或保存失败时，仍可查看已选日期的内容，并明确提示历史记录失败。
