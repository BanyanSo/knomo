## Knomo 1.13.5

### Improvements

- Loading feedback: Keep brief first loads quiet and show lightweight skeletons after 250 ms in memo lists, mobile search, and statistics. Retain existing statistics and sidebar tags during background updates.
- Page feedback: Show load, refresh, and source-change issues beside the current page with retry actions. Preserve valid results in mobile search, Random reunion, and Shuffle day, and avoid repeating the same screen-reader announcement on redraw.
- Settings feedback: Show results beside settings and maintenance controls, including Daily heading changes, Time buoy, index rebuilding, Monthly retries, and legacy Trash recovery. Keep outcomes visible after redraws and reduce duplicate notices.
- Search and paging: Cancel outdated loads when changing filters, switching Time buoy tabs, or closing a view, preventing earlier responses from replacing the current results.

### Fixes

- Prevent incomplete or cancelled initial reads from being mistaken for an empty library or no search matches. Show guidance or retry actions for disabled Daily notes, unavailable settings, or failed reads, and keep Monthly archive issues separate from memo loading.
- Keep previous statistics visible after a refresh failure with a retry action, and offer a local retry when the initial sidebar tag load fails.
- Cancel delayed loading placeholders after completion, cancellation, page changes, or hiding the view, so old callbacks cannot replace current content.
- Open All notes on cold start or the first Knomo pane in a workspace. Retry transient Catalog snapshot changes without flashing a refresh failure. Place the caret at the draft's end when expanding the mobile composer, and preserve the current selection on other refocus actions.
- Apply a saved Time buoy setting to open views even when its index query fails.
- Stop background indexing, card refreshes, or delayed failures from blocking the next save or clearing a newer draft after Daily has already confirmed the save.
- After a successful restore with cleanup pending, show Retry cleanup and remove only the recovery copy on retry, without appending the memo again. Prevent repeated clicks from starting duplicate restores.
- When a restore's Daily write cannot be confirmed, retain the recovery copy and prompt the user to check Daily before retrying.
- Prevent late reference, copy, or restore results and notices from affecting a closed or replaced view. Report the actual reason when referencing or copying fails.

### 改进

- 加载反馈：列表、移动搜索和统计首屏短暂等待时保持安静，超过 250 毫秒再显示轻量骨架；后台更新时保留已有统计和侧栏标签。
- 页面反馈：在当前页面就地显示加载、刷新和来源变化问题，并提供重试入口；移动搜索、随机重逢和往日漫游保留仍有效的结果，同一问题重绘时不重复触发屏幕阅读器播报。
- 设置反馈：日记标题、浮标、索引重建、Monthly 重试和旧版回收站恢复等操作在对应设置区域显示结果，重绘后仍可查看，并减少重复通知。
- 搜索与分页：切换筛选条件、浮标标签页或关闭视图时取消过期读取，避免较早的响应覆盖当前结果。

### 修复

- 修复首次读取不完整或被取消时误显示空库、无匹配的问题；日记关闭、设置不可用或读取失败时显示指引或重试入口，Monthly 归档故障与正文加载分别呈现。
- 统计刷新失败时保留旧数据并显示重试入口；侧栏标签首次加载失败时结束忙碌状态，支持局部重试。
- 修复结果返回、请求取消、切换页面或隐藏视图后旧延迟回调再次显示加载占位的问题，避免覆盖当前内容。
- 冷启动或工作区首次打开 Knomo 时显示全部笔记；索引快照短暂变化时重新读取，避免闪现刷新失败提示；移动端展开草稿时将光标放在全文末尾，其他重新聚焦操作保留当前选区。
- 浮标设置保存后，即使索引查询失败，也将新开关状态应用到已打开的视图。
- 修复 Daily 已确认保存后，后台索引、卡片刷新或迟到失败仍阻塞下一次保存、清空新草稿的问题。
- 正文恢复成功但副本清理未完成时显示“重试清理”，重试仅移除恢复副本，不重复追加 Memo；防止重复点击发起多次恢复。
- 恢复操作无法确认 Daily 写入时保留恢复副本，并明确提示先核对 Daily 再重试。
- 防止迟到的引用、复制或恢复结果及通知影响已经关闭或切换会话的视图，引用和复制失败时保留实际错误原因。
