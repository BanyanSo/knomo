## Knomo 1.13.6

### New features

- Add “View this day” to card menus on the Random revisit page. Open the full list of memos from the card's date, then return to the original cards and reading position.
- Add glass styling to the mobile header, bottom navigation, and create/send buttons. The companion Reduce glass effects preference lets this device use solid backgrounds while preserving theme colors and interaction states.
- Add Empty Trash to permanently clear all deleted memos after confirmation, from either the Trash page or a command.
- Add grouping to Beyond today, organizing older memos by year and quarter or half-year.

### Improvements

- Improve date selection in Shuffle day and random memo selection in Random revisit.
- Refine interface styling, unify font sizes, and improve controls, data refreshes, and feedback.

### Fixes

- History browsing: Preserve navigation and retry buttons during updates so the first tap is not lost. Restore mobile page focus when opening or leaving day details.
- Day details: Update the day's list and counts after memo changes. Until a complete read succeeds, additions or restores trigger a full reread; failures retain the error and retry action.
- Startup and Daily settings: Open All notes on cold start and distinguish incomplete results from an empty library. Offer guidance or retry when Daily is unavailable, and pause dependent writes when its configuration cannot be confirmed.
- Saving: Release the composer once Daily confirms a save. Protect newer drafts from late failures, and apply saved Time buoy settings to open views even if their query fails.
- Mobile input: Place the caret at the draft's end on expansion and preserve selection on refocus. Fix tag/link suggestion taps that move the caret, dismiss the composer, or activate controls underneath, while retaining list scrolling.
- Mobile date picker: Prevent overlapping date touch targets on narrow screens.
- Search and pagination: Cancel outdated loads when filters or pages change, and revalidate results after memo or index changes before loading more. Discard stale pages and counts while preserving unchanged cards, loaded images, and scroll position.
- Restore: Prevent duplicate restores. Retry only recovery-copy cleanup after a confirmed restore; retain the copy and prompt a Daily check when the write is unconfirmed.
- Monthly archive settings: Distinguish an unconfirmed filename change from a saved setting with incomplete archive regeneration. Retain completed archives and report remaining failures instead of showing success.
- Late results: Ignore stale reference, copy, and restore results after views close or change. Cancel outdated loading placeholders and preserve actual failure reasons.

### 新增功能

- 随机重逢页面的卡片菜单增加 ‘查看这一天’ 功能，可打开卡片对应当天完整的 Memos 列表，返回时保留原卡片内容阅读位置。
- 移动端的顶栏、底部导航、新建、发送按钮新增玻璃质感，配套本机“减少玻璃效果”开关，可改用实色背景，保留主题色与操作状态。
- 回收站新增全部清空功能，支持从回收站页面或命令确认后永久清理已删除的 Memos 。
- 今日之外新增分组显示，较早的记录按年份与季度或半年分组。

### 改进

- 改进往日漫游的日期选择算法，改进随机重逢的随机选取 Memos 的算法。
- 改进界面样式、字号统一以、优化控件样式、数据刷新、提示等交互体验。

### 修复

- 历史浏览：更新时保留导航和重试按钮，避免首次轻触失效；进入或返回日期详情后恢复移动端页面焦点。
- 日期详情：Memos 变化后同步当天列表与统计；整日尚未读取成功时，新增或恢复会触发完整重读，失败时保留错误和重试入口。
- 启动与日记配置：冷启动打开全部笔记，区分结果不完整与真实空库；日记不可用时提供指引或重试，配置无法确认时暂停相关写入。
- 保存：Daily 确认落盘后即可继续使用输入框；迟到失败不影响新草稿，浮标设置保存后即使查询失败也应用到已打开视图。
- 移动输入：展开草稿时将光标放在末尾，重新聚焦时保留选区；修复点选标签／链接候选导致光标跳动、输入层关闭或误触下方控件的问题，保留列表滚动。
- 移动日期面板：避免窄屏下日期触控区域重叠。
- 搜索与分页：筛选或页面变化时取消过期读取，Memos 或索引变化后先确认结果再加载更多；丢弃过期页面和计数，保留未变化的卡片、已加载图片与滚动位置。
- 恢复：防止重复恢复；正文已确认恢复时，重试仅清理副本；写入未确认时保留恢复副本，提示先核对日记。
- 月度归档设置：区分文件名更改未确认与设置已保存但归档重建未完成，保留已完成归档并明确提示剩余失败，避免误报成功。
- 迟到结果：视图关闭或切换后忽略过期引用、复制和恢复结果，取消旧加载占位，并保留实际失败原因。
