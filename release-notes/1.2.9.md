## Knomo 1.2.9

### Improvements

* Improved and standardized bottom spacing across feature pages to prevent the last card from being obscured, and refined the card menu styling.
* Simplified Memo reference aliases into a more readable date-and-time format while maintaining compatibility with existing references.
* Expanded custom date headings for monthly Memos. The format now supports YYYY, M, MM, MMM, D, DD, MMMM, and dddd tokens, including localized month and weekday names, while preserving Markdown and WikiLink syntax.

---

### 改进

* 改进、统一各个功能页面的底部留白，避免底部卡片被遮挡，并优化卡片菜单样式。 
* 简化 Memo 引用别名为易读的日期时间格式，同时保持对既有引用的解析兼容性。
* 扩展月度 Memo 自定义日期标题，现支持 YYYY、M、MM、D、DD、MMM 和 dddd 标记，可显示本地化的月份和星期名称，并保留 Markdown 与 WikiLink 语法。
